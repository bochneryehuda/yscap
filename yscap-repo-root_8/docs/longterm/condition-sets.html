Mortgages on the credit report — a statement for each

Borrower sees itDocument upload

Read the liabilities off the credit report, mark which are mortgages and which are not, and collect a current statement for every mortgage. A mortgage that is the borrower’s own primary residence can be linked to it instead of uploaded again; anything else needs its own statement against the property it is secured by.

They see it as Statements for your other mortgages

Applies to Every file.

Each mortgage is answered one of three ways — not a form to fill in:

  • Upload a statement for this mortgage (nothing further)
  • This is the mortgage on the home they live in (nothing further)
  • Say which property it is secured by (property address, investment or second home, monthly rent, the last only when it applies)

Vesting entity — formation documents and ownership

Borrower sees itDocument upload

The entity taking title: its articles, its operating agreement or bylaws, its EIN letter and who its members are. Verified once and then verified forever — the entity lives on the borrower’s profile, so a second loan for the same entity starts already done.

They see it as Your company’s documents

Applies to Title is taken in an entity is yes.

  • Articles of formation
  • Operating agreement or bylaws
  • EIN letter
  • Certificate of good standing (optional)

Mortgage statement on the subject property

Borrower sees itDocument upload

A current statement on the loan being paid off. Three ways to satisfy it: the statement itself; the payoff figures typed in — outstanding balance, servicer AND loan number, all three, none of them optional; or a waiver where the loan being refinanced is one of our own short-term loans serviced by FCI, where we already hold everything a statement would say.

They see it as Your current mortgage statement

Applies to Is a refinance is yes.

Answered one of three ways — a document is only one of them:

  • Upload the mortgage statement (nothing further)
  • Type the loan in instead (outstanding principal balance, servicer, loan number)
  • This refinances one of our own short-term loans, serviced by FCI (nothing further)

Internal onlyDocuSign

Housing history verified

Internal onlyDocument upload

One of three, decided by what the borrower said about where they live (FR0115): the rent verification back from the landlord if they rent, a mortgage verification on their own home if they own it, or a letter if they live somewhere rent free. They are alternatives, not a list — asking for all three would be asking for two things that cannot exist.

Applies to Every file.

  • Verification of rent (completed) (optional, only when Borrower rents where they live)
  • Verification of mortgage — their own home (optional, only when Borrower owns where they live)
  • Living rent free letter (optional, only when Borrower lives rent free)

Two things worth knowing

Nothing here is fixed in code. Every condition’s wording, the rule that decides which files get it, whether the borrower sees it, and whether it applies at all are all edited on the Condition Center settings screen. What ships is the pre-fill — our own rules of the road — not a limit.

A condition the borrower sees is written twice. The internal wording is what our team reads; the borrower is shown their own version, and their screen is built from that rather than by hiding parts of ours. That is why an internal note can never leak onto their screen.
