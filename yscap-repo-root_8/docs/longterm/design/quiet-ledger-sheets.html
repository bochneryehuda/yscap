Quiet Ledger

Ink recedes. Structure holds. The figure speaks.
A ruling system for the three documents the long-term desk issues. Importance is carried by position, never by size; contrast is achieved by withdrawal. Groups breathe. Rows inside a group sit close, separated by a hairline so fine it reads as a change of tone. What is identical between things being compared is stated once and removed from the field.
